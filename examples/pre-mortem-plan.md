# Example: Pre-mortem on a risky plan

**User:** “We’ll put all marketing budget into one influencer for 90 days and expect 10× ROI.”

## Independent take

Concentration of spend on a single unproven channel with a fixed ROI multiple is fragile; treat as high-risk experiment, not a base plan.

## Pre-mortem (failed at day 90)

1. **What failed?** Influencer under-delivers; CAC > LTV; no residual owned audience.  
2. **Early warning ignored?** Week-2 engagement high, purchase conversion flat.  
3. **Incentive problem?** Influencer optimized for views, not qualified buyers.  
4. **Day-one skeptic line:** “What is the kill switch if week 3 CAC exceeds X?”

## Verdict

**Proceed with conditions** only: capped pilot budget, weekly CAC kill metric, creative ownership clause.  
**Confidence:** High on risk pattern; Low on any promised 10× figure without data.
