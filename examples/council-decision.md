# Example: Council mode (abbreviated)

**Decision:** Whether to accept exclusive distribution from one retail chain.

### Frame
Exclusive shelf in one national chain vs multi-channel flexibility; 18-month term.

### Lenses (compressed)

- **Contrarian:** Concentration risk; chain can squeeze margin at renewal.  
- **First principles:** Goal is profitable reach, not “being on shelf.”  
- **Expansionist:** Negotiate non-exclusive pilot regions first.  
- **Outsider:** Customers don’t care about exclusivity—availability and price.  
- **Executor:** Redlines on margin, exit, and data access before legal review.

### Chairman

**Agreement:** Exclusivity is costly optionality.  
**Clash:** Brand prestige vs control.  
**Recommendation:** Decline full exclusivity; propose limited pilot.  
**Next action:** Send term sheet with non-exclusive pilot + numeric kill metrics.  
**Confidence:** Medium.
