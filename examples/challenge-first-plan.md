# Example: Challenge-first on a product plan

**User (summary):** “We’ll launch an AI resume rewriter next month, charge $29/mo, grow only via TikTok, and hit $50k MRR in 90 days.”

## Strongest charitable summary

A subscription tool that rewrites resumes with AI, monetized at $29/month, with customer acquisition concentrated on TikTok, targeting $50k MRR within 90 days of launch.

## Assumptions

- TikTok can produce enough **intent-aligned** demand (not only views).  
- $29/mo is acceptable vs free chatbots and existing career tools.  
- Rewrite quality is differentiated enough to retain month 2+.  
- 90-day $50k MRR (~1,700 paying accounts if pure sub) is operationally reachable.

## What holds

Clear offer and price point; short feedback loop if shipping is real.

## Critical findings

1. **Acquisition channel risk (severity 4)** — TikTok reach ≠ paid conversion for career tools; need measured CAC, not vibes.  
2. **Retention unknown (severity 4)** — One-shot rewrite may not support monthly billing without ongoing value.  
3. **Timeline math (severity 3)** — $50k MRR in 90 days implies aggressive paid or viral efficiency rarely seen cold-start.

## Strongest counter-argument

Users will try a free model once, not subscribe monthly unless the product is embedded in a recurring workflow (job applications tracker, tailored variants, alerts).

## Verdict

**Revise substantially** before treating $50k/90 days as a plan. Pilot retention and CAC on a smaller target.

**Confidence:** Medium on qualitative risks; Low on the numeric forecast (no funnel data).
